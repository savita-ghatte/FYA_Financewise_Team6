Understanding Digital Payments

📱 What are Digital Payments?

Electronic transfer of money without cash.

⚡ Benefits

Fast, secure, 24×7 and convenient.

🌍 Uses

Shopping, bills, recharges, tickets and fees.

Types of Digital Payments

UPI

Instant QR and bank transfers.

Debit Card

Pay directly from your bank account.

Credit Card

Spend now, pay later.

Net Banking

Transfer funds through your bank website.

Payment Process

Scan QR
Enter Amount
