Understanding Insurance

🛡️ What is Insurance?

Insurance is a financial agreement that helps cover losses in exchange for a premium.

💡 Why Insurance?

It provides financial security during emergencies and unexpected events.

📄 Premium & Claim

A premium is the amount you pay. A claim is the request for financial support.
