Budgeting

Budgeting is the process of planning how you will spend and save your money. A good budget helps you control expenses, avoid unnecessary spending, achieve your financial goals, and prepare for emergencies.

Understanding Budgeting

📋 What is a Budget?

A budget is a financial plan that shows how much money you earn, spend, and save over a specific period such as a week or month.

🎯 Why Budget?

Budgeting helps you avoid overspending, save for future goals, manage unexpected expenses, and reduce financial stress.

💰 Income vs Expenses

Income is the money you receive, while expenses are the money you spend. Your income should always be greater than or equal to your expenses.

Steps to Create a Budget

1️⃣ Calculate Income

Know exactly how much money you receive every month.

2️⃣ List Expenses

Write down fixed and variable expenses like food, transport, and entertainment.

3️⃣ Save First

Keep a portion of your income aside before spending.

4️⃣ Review Monthly

Track your spending and improve your budget every month.

Popular 50 • 30 • 20 Budget Rule

50%

Needs like food, rent, education, transport, and bills.

30%

Wants like shopping, movies, eating out, and hobbies.

20%

Savings and investments for your future goals.

Smart Budgeting Tips

📝 Track Every Expense

Recording every expense helps you understand where your money goes.

🎯 Set Financial Goals

Save money for education, gadgets, travel, or emergencies with a clear goal.

💳 Avoid Impulse Buying

Wait before making unnecessary purchases. Ask yourself if you really need the item.
