Understanding Banking

🏦 What is a Bank?

A bank is a financial institution that keeps your money safe and provides financial services.

💳 Types of Accounts

Savings, Current, Fixed Deposit (FD), and Recurring Deposit (RD).

💵 Banking Services

Deposit money, withdraw cash, transfer funds, pay bills, and apply for loans.

Banking Safety Tips

🔒 Keep PIN Secret

Never share your ATM PIN or OTP.

📱 Verify Apps

Use only official banking apps and websites.

⚠ Beware of Scams

Ignore suspicious calls asking for bank details.
